# Design System: Pace Setter International
 
## Core Identity
Pace Setter International is a premier educational and career consultancy service guiding students on their global study abroad journey. The design evokes trust, prestige, authority, and global connectivity.

## Color Palette
- **Primary**: #032B5A (Deep Royal Navy Blue - Authority & Trust)
- **Primary Light**: #0A427E (Royal Navy - Hover & Focus)
- **Primary Dark**: #021A37 (Midnight Navy - Depth & Contrast)
- **Secondary**: #0284C7 (Ocean Blue - Innovation & Connectivity)
- **Secondary Light**: #38BDF8 (Light Sky Blue - Soft Highlights)
- **Accent**: #16A34A (Vibrant Emerald Green - Growth, Success & Action)
- **Accent Light**: #22C55E (Fresh Emerald - Hover Highlight)
- **Accent Dark**: #15803D (Deep Emerald)
- **Background**: #FFFFFF (Clean White)
- **Background Soft**: #F8FAFC (Soft Cool Slate)
- **Text**: #0B1B2B (Deep Navy Charcoal)

## Typography
- **Primary Font**: 'Inter', sans-serif
- **Headings**: Semibold/Bold, tracking tight
- **Body**: Regular, optimal line height (1.6)

## Component Guidelines
- **Buttons**: Rounded (full), shadow on hover, transition 0.3s.
- **Cards**: Soft borders (1px solid #e2e8f0), subtle shadow, padding 2rem.
- **Hero Section**: High-impact imagery with clear CTA.
- **Navigation**: Sticky, transparent to solid on scroll.

## Layout
- **Container**: Max-width 1152px (max-w-6xl).
- **Spacing**: Consistent 4rem - 8rem section padding.
- **Responsive**: Mobile-first grid (1 col -> 3 col).

## Animations
- Fade-in on scroll for sections.
- Subtle hover scale for service cards.
- Smooth page transitions.
